## Introduction

An affiliate click does not earn commission indefinitely. Every affiliate programme defines an attribution window—often called a [cookie window]({{ 'cookie-window/' | relative_url }}) or cookie duration—that determines how long a referral remains eligible for commission after a visitor clicks an affiliate link. For website owners, this rule can make the difference between a profitable article and one that generates little income, particularly when readers research products over several days or weeks before buying.


<img src="{{ "/assets/images/making_money_from_cr_f649af_commission_models_51e452_cookie_windows_330e2f-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Cookie Windows illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/making_money_from_cr_f649af_commission_models_51e452_cookie_windows_330e2f-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/making_money_from_cr_f649af_commission_models_51e452_cookie_windows_330e2f-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
Cookie windows work alongside basket or shopping cart rules, which determine whether adding products to a basket within the tracking period extends commission eligibility. These policies vary significantly between merchants, so understanding them is just as important as comparing headline commission rates. A programme offering a generous percentage commission may still produce lower earnings than a competitor if its attribution rules are much stricter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avelonetwork.com/support/brand/affiliate-attribution-windows-first-party-cookies" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avelonetwork.com">[Avelon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avelonetwork.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avelon Understanding Affiliate Attribution Windows</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AvelonUnderstanding Affiliate Attribution WindowsNovember 23, 2024 — An affiliate attribution window, otherwise known as the cookie durat...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 23, 2024</span></span></span>

## What an Attribution Window Does

An attribution window is the period during which a merchant agrees to credit an affiliate for a qualifying purchase after a customer clicks an affiliate link. If the purchase happens before the window expires and all programme conditions are met, the affiliate receives commission. If the purchase happens afterwards, the referral normally expires unless another rule extends it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avelonetwork.com/support/brand/affiliate-attribution-windows-first-party-cookies" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avelonetwork.com">[Avelon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avelonetwork.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avelon Understanding Affiliate Attribution Windows</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AvelonUnderstanding Affiliate Attribution WindowsNovember 23, 2024 — An affiliate attribution window, otherwise known as the cookie durat...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 23, 2024</span></span></span>

Cookie durations differ because different products have different buying journeys.

* **Short windows (24–48 hours)** are common for fast-moving consumer goods and marketplaces where buyers often purchase immediately.
* **Medium windows (around 30 days)** are widely used for standard ecommerce because they accommodate typical comparison shopping.
* **Long windows (60–90 days or longer)** are more common for [software]({{ 'software/' | relative_url }}), expensive products, business services, or purchases requiring extended research and approval.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://trackdesk.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-cookie-duration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trackdesk.com">[Trackdesk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trackdesk.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cookie Duration in Affiliate Marketing: Find Your “Sweet...13 Mar 2025 — Short Duration (24-48 hours): Found in Amazon Associat...</span></span></span>

The window reflects a commercial decision rather than a technical limitation. Merchants balance rewarding affiliates against limiting how long they remain financially responsible for a referral.

A longer attribution window generally benefits content publishers whose visitors spend time comparing products, reading [reviews]({{ 'reviews/' | relative_url }}) and returning later to complete a purchase. Conversely, a very short window favours affiliates who reach consumers already close to making a buying decision.

## How Basket Rules Affect Ecommerce Commissions

The cookie window is only one part of the attribution policy. Many ecommerce programmes also define basket rules that determine whether adding products to a shopping cart changes the commission outcome.

[Amazon Associates]({{ 'amazon/' | relative_url }}) provides one of the best-known examples. Its standard referral window lasts 24 hours after a visitor arrives through an affiliate link. However, if a qualifying item is added to the customer's basket during that 24-hour period, commission may still be earned if that specific item is purchased before the basket expires, typically within about 90 days. This extension applies only to items added during the qualifying window rather than to later purchases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.co.uk">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Amazon Associates Amazon.co.uk Associates Central</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesAmazon.co.uk Associates Central - HelpAs long as the item was added to the customer&#x27;s Shopping Cart during this 24-hour...</span></span></span>

This distinction creates several practical consequences:

* A visitor who buys immediately remains within the standard attribution window.
* A visitor who adds the product to their basket within the qualifying period but checks out weeks later may still generate commission.
* Products added after the original window has expired usually do not qualify under the extended basket rule.
* Returning through another affiliate's qualifying link or completing an order may end the original attribution period, depending on programme rules.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.com">[affiliate-program.amazon.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">24 hours You earn commission income on any qualifying items placed in a customer&#x27;s Shopping Cart within 24 hours of their arrival at Amaz</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24 hoursYou earn commission income on any qualifying items placed in a customer&#x27;s Shopping Cart within 24 hours of their arrival at Amazo...</span></span></span>

Basket rules therefore matter most for shoppers who delay payment after deciding what to buy, rather than for people who postpone the entire purchase decision.

## Why Short Windows Change Content Strategy

Cookie duration directly influences the type of content that performs well.

With a 24-hour attribution window, content works best when it reaches readers who already intend to purchase. Examples include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* product comparison pages aimed at buyers choosing between final options;
* seasonal buying guides during major sales events;
* deal pages highlighting limited-time discounts;
* articles answering last-minute purchasing questions.

</div>

Long-form educational articles introducing unfamiliar products may generate many clicks but relatively few [commissions]({{ 'commissions/' | relative_url }}) if readers return days later through another channel after the original cookie has expired.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://trackdesk.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-cookie-duration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trackdesk.com">[Trackdesk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trackdesk.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cookie Duration in Affiliate Marketing: Find Your “Sweet...13 Mar 2025 — Short Duration (24-48 hours): Found in Amazon Associat...</span></span></span>

Longer attribution windows, by contrast, reward content that participates earlier in the buying journey. Detailed tutorials, comprehensive reviews, industry comparisons and informational guides become more valuable because affiliates can still receive credit when readers return weeks later to complete the purchase.

## Why Attribution Rules Matter More Than the Headline Commission

Two programmes paying the same percentage can produce very different earnings because their tracking policies differ.

Imagine two merchants each offering a 10% commission:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* Merchant A uses a 24-hour attribution window.
* Merchant B uses a 60-day attribution window.

</div>

If most visitors buy within hours, the programmes may perform similarly. However, if customers typically spend several weeks researching before purchasing, Merchant B is likely to attribute substantially more sales to the affiliate.

The opposite can also occur. A short-window programme may outperform a long-window programme when promoting inexpensive products that customers buy immediately after reading a review.

This is why experienced affiliate publishers evaluate the complete commission model rather than comparing percentages alone.

These rules explain why two affiliates sending apparently similar [traffic]({{ 'traffic/' | relative_url }}) may see different commission results.

## Choosing Programmes with Cookie Policies in Mind

When comparing affiliate programmes, cookie duration should be assessed alongside commission rate, average order value and conversion performance.

Questions worth checking include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* How many days does the attribution window last?
* Is there a basket or shopping cart extension?
* Does another affiliate's click replace the original attribution?
* Are there different cookie durations for different product categories?
* Does the typical customer buy immediately or after extensive research?

</div>
