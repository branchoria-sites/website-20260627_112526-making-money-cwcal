## Introduction

Paid advertising is unforgiving in affiliate marketing because the visitor must usually complete two separate conversions before any commission is earned. First, the advertisement must persuade someone to visit the affiliate's landing page. Second, that visitor must click through to the merchant, complete a purchase, survive any validation checks, and have the sale approved for commission. Every additional step introduces another opportunity for potential customers to leave the funnel, making paid affiliate campaigns far less tolerant of weak conversion rates than businesses that own their own checkout.

This "two-step" structure is one of the main reasons profitable-looking paid campaigns can still lose money. An affiliate pays for every advertising click immediately, yet only earns revenue if enough users successfully move through both the affiliate page and the merchant's purchasing process. The affiliate can optimise only the first part of that journey while having limited influence over everything that follows.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://remoby.com/blog/how-affiliate-marketing-works-from-click-to-commission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[Remoby+2wecantrack]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to Commission</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to CommissionMay 7, 2026 — 4 Jun 2026 — Learn how affiliate marketing works step by step...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 2026</span></span></span>

## The Hidden Drop-Off in Paid Affiliate Funnels

Unlike an online shop that controls its own product page and checkout, an affiliate publisher hands the visitor to another business before the transaction is complete. This creates a sequential funnel:

1. Advertisement → affiliate landing page.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://remoby.com/blog/affiliate-landing-page-strategy-framework/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[remoby.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Affiliate Landing Page Strategy Framework: Choose the...March 27, 2026 — 8 Jun 2026 — This affiliate landing page strategy framewo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 27, 2026</span></span></span> 2. Affiliate landing page → merchant website.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://remoby.com/blog/affiliate-landing-page-strategy-framework/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[remoby.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Affiliate Landing Page Strategy Framework: Choose the...March 27, 2026 — 8 Jun 2026 — This affiliate landing page strategy framewo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 27, 2026</span></span></span>
3. Merchant website → completed and approved commission.

Each stage has its own conversion rate. The overall probability of earning a commission is the product of all three, not simply the success of any individual stage.

For example, suppose:

* 100 people click a paid advert.
* 35% click from the affiliate page to the merchant.
* 4% of those visitors complete a purchase.
* 90% of purchases are ultimately approved for commission.

The affiliate earns [commissions]({{ 'commissions/' | relative_url }}) from approximately 1.26 customers (100 × 35% × 4% × 90%). Even modest declines at any stage reduce total revenue disproportionately because the losses multiply rather than add together.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://remoby.com/blog/how-affiliate-marketing-works-from-click-to-commission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[Remoby]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to Commission</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to CommissionMay 7, 2026 — 4 Jun 2026 — Learn how affiliate marketing works step by step...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 2026</span></span></span>

## Ad Click to Landing-Page Visitor

The first conversion is the one the affiliate controls most directly. Advertising platforms measure whether users click the advert, but that says nothing about whether the [traffic]({{ 'traffic/' | relative_url }}) is commercially valuable.

A paid visitor arrives with expectations shaped by the advert. If the landing page fails to match those expectations, several problems emerge immediately:

* visitors leave without reading;
* trust declines because the page feels misleading or low quality;
* the outbound affiliate link receives few clicks;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/what-is-affiliate-link-ctr-in-ecommerce-how-to-improve-it/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: alexanderjarvis.com">[alexanderjarvis.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">alexanderjarvis.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ant to aim for 1-5% CTR to guarantee...Read more...</span></span></span>
* advertising spend is wasted before the merchant even has a chance to sell.

This is particularly dangerous because every visitor already has an acquisition cost attached. Organic visitors who bounce represent lost opportunity; paid visitors represent an immediate cash loss.

Effective affiliate landing pages therefore focus less on maximising raw page views and more on qualifying visitors before sending them onward. Clear product comparisons, transparent [reviews]({{ 'reviews/' | relative_url }}), realistic pricing information and honest disclosure help ensure that those who click through are genuinely interested rather than merely curious. High-quality traffic often produces higher [earnings per click]({{ 'earnings-per-click/' | relative_url }}) (EPC) than simply increasing outbound click volume.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://remoby.com/blog/affiliate-landing-page-strategy-framework/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[Remoby+2wecantrack]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Affiliate Landing Page Strategy Framework: Choose the...March 27, 2026 — 8 Jun 2026 — This affiliate landing page strategy framewo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 27, 2026</span></span></span>

## Landing-Page Click-Through to the Merchant

The second conversion is unique to affiliate marketing. A visitor must trust the affiliate enough to leave the site and continue elsewhere.

Every outbound click asks the visitor to make another decision:

* continue researching;
* abandon the purchase;
* search independently for the merchant;
* compare competing products;
* click the affiliate link.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://remoby.com/blog/how-affiliate-marketing-works-from-click-to-commission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[remoby.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to Commission</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to CommissionMay 7, 2026 — 4 Jun 2026 — Learn how affiliate marketing works step by step...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 2026</span></span></span>

Many users disappear during this transition. Some decide to postpone buying. Others open multiple [comparison sites]({{ 'comparisons/' | relative_url }}). Some search Google for the merchant directly, potentially changing attribution depending on programme rules and attribution windows.

This explains why affiliate pages should not simply chase the highest possible click-through rate. Sending poorly qualified users to the merchant may increase outbound clicks while reducing EPC because few visitors actually purchase. Experienced affiliates often judge landing pages by profitable click quality rather than raw click quantity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://remoby.com/blog/affiliate-landing-page-strategy-framework/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[Remoby+2wecantrack]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Affiliate Landing Page Strategy Framework: Choose the...March 27, 2026 — 8 Jun 2026 — This affiliate landing page strategy framewo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 27, 2026</span></span></span>

Another practical limitation is that affiliates cannot usually redesign the merchant's destination page. If the retailer's product page loads slowly, presents confusing pricing, requires account creation, or has poor mobile usability, the affiliate bears the financial consequences despite having no [authority]({{ 'authority/' | relative_url }}) to correct those problems.

## Merchant Checkout Conversion and Commission Approval

Once the visitor reaches the merchant, most of the remaining funnel is outside the affiliate's control.

The merchant determines:

* product availability;
* pricing;
* delivery costs;
* checkout design;
* payment options;
* fraud screening;
* refund policies;
* commission validation rules.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://remoby.com/blog/how-affiliate-marketing-works-from-click-to-commission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[remoby.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to Commission</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to CommissionMay 7, 2026 — 4 Jun 2026 — Learn how affiliate marketing works step by step...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 2026</span></span></span>

A visitor who looked highly promising on the affiliate's landing page may still fail to complete a purchase because the merchant's checkout introduces unexpected friction. Even after a recorded conversion, commissions may remain pending or later be rejected because of cancellations, returns, duplicate transactions, invalid leads or programme policy violations. A tracked sale is therefore not automatically equivalent to payable revenue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://remoby.com/blog/how-affiliate-marketing-works-from-click-to-commission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: remoby.com">[Remoby]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">remoby.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to Commission</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How Affiliate Marketing Works: From Click to CommissionMay 7, 2026 — 4 Jun 2026 — Learn how affiliate marketing works step by step...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 7, 2026</span></span></span>

This uncertainty makes affiliate advertising fundamentally different from operating one's own ecommerce store. The affiliate purchases traffic with certainty but receives revenue only after multiple independent events have all succeeded.

## Why Paid Traffic Leaves Little Margin for Error

The mathematics of the funnel explain why affiliate campaigns often become unprofitable surprisingly quickly.

Imagine advertising costs £1 per click.

If 100 clicks cost £100:

* improving the [merchant conversion]({{ 'merchant-cr/' | relative_url }}) rate from 2% to 3% increases commissions by 50%;
* increasing landing-page click-through from 30% to 40% also has a large effect;
* but small declines in both stages multiply together, rapidly eroding profitability.

This compounding means affiliates usually need stronger economics than merchants who own their checkout. An ecommerce retailer can optimise advertising, landing pages, checkout, email recovery, promotions and customer support. The affiliate generally controls only traffic quality and the pre-sell experience before handing the visitor away.

Consequently, profitable paid affiliate campaigns tend to require one or more of the following:

* unusually high commission rates;
* merchants with proven conversion performance;
* inexpensive, highly targeted traffic;
* exceptionally persuasive pre-sell content;
* continuous testing of landing-page messaging and visitor intent.

Without those advantages, the two-step funnel leaves very little room to absorb advertising costs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wecantrack.com/insights/epc-in-affiliate-marketing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wecantrack.com">[wecantrack+2financeAds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wecantrack.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EPC in Affiliate Marketing: Calculate and Increase ItEarnings Per Click (EPC) is the most critical statistic in affiliate marke...</span></span></span>

## Measuring the Right Funnel Instead of a Single Conversion Rate

A common beginner mistake is evaluating only one metric, such as advertising click-through rate or affiliate link clicks.

The entire sequence should instead be viewed as connected stages:

* **Ad click rate** measures whether the advert attracts attention.
* **Landing-page engagement** indicates whether visitors remain interested.
* **Affiliate link click-through rate** shows whether the page creates sufficient trust to send visitors onward.
* **Merchant conversion rate** determines whether qualified visitors actually buy.
* **Commission approval rate** determines how much recorded revenue becomes payable.

Weak performance at any stage reduces the effectiveness of every preceding stage. A brilliantly optimised advert cannot compensate for a poor merchant checkout, while an excellent merchant cannot rescue traffic that leaves the affiliate page without clicking through.

Understanding this chain is central to managing paid affiliate campaigns. The affiliate is not buying customers directly; they are buying opportunities that must survive multiple independent conversions before generating revenue.
