## Introduction

[Amazon Associates]({{ 'amazon/' | relative_url }}) is often recommended to beginners because Amazon converts visitors into buyers remarkably well. However, high conversion alone does not guarantee worthwhile affiliate income. The amount earned from each sale depends on a combination of the commission category, the product price, the number of visitors who click, the proportion who buy, and whether the purchase qualifies under Amazon's attribution rules. Official commission schedules show that similar-looking products can earn very different percentages depending on their category, meaning two websites with identical [traffic]({{ 'traffic/' | relative_url }}) can produce dramatically different monthly income.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.com">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Associates Standard Commission Income Rates Table 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digital Mu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesStandard Commission Income RatesTable 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digit...</span></span></span>

For anyone building websites containing affiliate links, understanding this arithmetic is more valuable than chasing traffic alone. A niche with attractive buying intent but poor commission economics can require many times more visitors than one with a stronger earnings equation.

## How category rates change the earnings equation

Amazon no longer pays a single percentage across its catalogue. Instead, products are grouped into categories with fixed commission rates. While the exact schedule varies between Amazon marketplaces and can change over time, the principle remains the same: what the customer buys matters just as much as whether they buy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.com">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Associates Standard Commission Income Rates Table 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digital Mu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesStandard Commission Income RatesTable 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digit...</span></span></span>

For example, categories commonly sit across a broad range:

* Grocery and some gaming-related categories may earn around 1%.
* Electronics and similar products often fall into lower percentage bands.
* Home, sports and many everyday consumer products frequently sit around the middle.
* Books and kitchen products generally pay more than basic groceries.
* Luxury beauty and selected premium categories can offer substantially higher percentages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.com">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Associates Standard Commission Income Rates Table 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digital Mu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesStandard Commission Income RatesTable 1 – Fixed Standard Commission Income Rates for Specific Product Categories ; Digit...</span></span></span>

The practical consequence is that 100 sales are not equal. Selling 100 products in a low-rate category may produce only a fraction of the commission generated by the same number of sales in a higher-rate category.

This explains why revenue comparisons between affiliate websites can be misleading. One publisher may appear to have "only" a few thousand monthly visitors but covers products with favourable commission rates, while another receives far more traffic yet struggles because its niche sits in one of Amazon's weakest-paying categories.

## Why product price matters as much as conversion

Commission percentages tell only half the story. The other half is the value of the products being purchased.

A simple earnings formula illustrates the relationship:

**Monthly earnings = Visitors × Click-through rate × Conversion rate × Average product price × Commission percentage**

Every element affects the final outcome.

Consider two simplified examples.

SiteAverage productCommissionCommission per saleBudget grocery site£201%£0.20Kitchen equipment site£2504.5%£11.25

Even if both websites generate exactly 100 completed purchases in a month, the first earns approximately £20 while the second earns about £1,125.

Neither example guarantees real-world results, but the arithmetic demonstrates why product value and commission percentage multiply together. Small improvements in both variables can outweigh large increases in traffic.

## Why Amazon's strong conversion cannot overcome weak economics

Amazon enjoys exceptional consumer trust. Many shoppers already have accounts, saved payment methods and confidence in delivery and returns. That familiarity often produces higher conversion rates than unfamiliar online retailers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GWJ4AHCH7U5LCL86?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.co.uk">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Amazon Associates What items qualify for commission income, and how ...Associates earn commission income when visitors follow links from</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesWhat items qualify for commission income, and how ...Associates earn commission income when visitors follow links from t...</span></span></span>

However, strong conversion has limits.

Suppose one niche converts twice as well as another:

* Site A converts 10% of clicks but earns 1% commission.
* Site B converts 5% of clicks but earns 8% commission through another merchant.

The higher conversion rate does not automatically produce higher earnings because each completed sale is worth so much less.

For beginners, this is one of the easiest mistakes to make. A dashboard showing hundreds of ordered items can feel encouraging until the monthly commission statement reveals that each order generated only a few pence.

## Which beginner niches face weak payout maths

Some beginner-friendly topics naturally produce weaker Amazon earnings because they combine several unfavourable factors.

Common examples include:

* Low-priced household consumables.
* Grocery-focused recommendation sites.
* Cheap [accessories]({{ 'accessories/' | relative_url }}) with very small order values.
* Categories assigned relatively low commission percentages.
* Niches where customers typically purchase only one inexpensive item.

These sites can still succeed, particularly if they achieve very large search traffic or exceptionally high purchase frequency, but they require much greater scale before earnings become meaningful.

By contrast, niches involving moderately expensive products—such as specialist kitchen equipment, quality tools, premium home products or enthusiast hobbies—often produce healthier mathematics because individual [commissions]({{ 'commissions/' | relative_url }}) are larger even if conversion rates are slightly lower.

## Attribution rules also affect the final earnings

The commission calculation does not end once someone clicks a link.

Amazon only pays commission on qualifying purchases made under its programme rules. Orders must meet eligibility requirements, and commissions are calculated from the qualifying sale price rather than a product's original list price. Different product types may also be excluded or assigned zero commission depending on programme rules and marketplace policies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GWJ4AHCH7U5LCL86?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amazon.co.uk">[Amazon Associates]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amazon.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Amazon Associates What items qualify for commission income, and how ...Associates earn commission income when visitors follow links from</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amazon AssociatesWhat items qualify for commission income, and how ...Associates earn commission income when visitors follow links from t...</span></span></span>

For beginners, this means the apparent value of clicks in analytics can exceed the value of commissions received. Traffic is only the first stage; eligible purchases determine income.

## The practical lesson for new affiliate websites

Amazon Associates remains an excellent benchmark for learning affiliate marketing because its conversion rates and consumer trust reduce many early barriers. The important lesson, however, is that traffic should never be evaluated in isolation.

Before committing to a niche, estimate the complete earnings equation:

* Expected commission category.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA?tag=searcht-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: affiliate-program.amazon.com">[affiliate-program.amazon.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">affiliate-program.amazon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxury Beauty, Luxury Stores Beauty, Amazon Coins, 5.00%. Furniture, Home, Home Improvement, Lawn &amp; Garden...Read more...</span></span></span>
* Typical selling price.
* Likely click-through rate.
* Expected purchase conversion.
* Approximate commission per completed sale.

</div>

Understanding that arithmetic early helps beginners avoid building large, busy websites whose underlying [commission maths]({{ 'commission-maths/' | relative_url }}) was never favourable to begin with.
