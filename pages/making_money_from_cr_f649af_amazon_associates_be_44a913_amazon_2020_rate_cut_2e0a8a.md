## Introduction

For many beginners, [Amazon Associates]({{ 'amazon/' | relative_url }}) is the first affiliate programme they encounter. That familiarity can create a false sense of security. The commission cuts announced in April 2020 demonstrated that even the largest and most trusted affiliate programme can change its payout structure with little notice, immediately affecting publishers whose income depended heavily on Amazon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://searchengineland.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-cut-332966" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: searchengineland.com">[Search Engine Land]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">searchengineland.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">amazon affiliate commission rates cut 332966</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Engine LandBig Amazon affiliate commission rate cuts among latest...Apr 15, 2020 — The Amazon Associates program is set to cut co...</span></span></span>

The lesson was not that Amazon became unusable. Rather, it showed why a website built around affiliate links should avoid relying on a single merchant for [long-term]({{ 'long-term/' | relative_url }}) revenue. A business that earns almost all of its income from one commission table is exposed to decisions it cannot influence, regardless of how much [traffic]({{ 'traffic/' | relative_url }}) or trust it has built.

## What changed in the 2020 commission cuts

* Furniture, Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Pet Products and Pantry items falling from 8% to 3%.
* Beauty products falling from 6% to 3%.
* Grocery products falling from 5% to 1%.

## Why merchant dependence creates fragile revenue

The 2020 changes highlighted an important business risk that is easy for beginners to overlook.

A successful affiliate website does not merely depend on search traffic or content quality. It also depends on the commercial agreements behind every click. If a single retailer changes those terms, months or years of optimisation may suddenly produce much less income.

Consider two otherwise identical websites:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Both receive 100,000 monthly visitors.
* Both convert readers into buyers at similar rates.
* Both recommend products in the same niche.

</div>

If one website earns 95% of its affiliate income through Amazon, a commission reduction immediately affects almost the entire business. Another publisher with the same audience but income spread across several merchants experiences a much smaller shock because losses from one programme are partly offset by others.

This is why experienced affiliates often measure *revenue concentration* alongside traffic. A site with diversified income is generally more resilient than one relying on a single merchant, even if current earnings are identical.

The 2020 cuts also demonstrated that strong conversion rates cannot fully compensate for lower [commissions]({{ 'commissions/' | relative_url }}). Amazon remains a highly trusted retailer that often converts visitors well, but excellent conversion cannot always overcome a large reduction in the percentage paid to the publisher.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://searchengineland.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-cut-332966" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: searchengineland.com">[Search Engine Land]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">searchengineland.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">amazon affiliate commission rates cut 332966</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Engine LandBig Amazon affiliate commission rate cuts among latest...Apr 15, 2020 — The Amazon Associates program is set to cut co...</span></span></span>

## When to test specialist retailers or direct partnerships

The practical response to the 2020 experience was not necessarily to abandon Amazon. Instead, many publishers began treating it as one option among several.

As a website gains authority, it becomes easier to compare alternative merchants for the same products. Specialist retailers may offer:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Higher commission percentages.
* Longer attribution or cookie windows.
* Better support from affiliate managers.
* Exclusive promotions or discount codes.
* More stable commercial relationships in particular product categories.

</div>

Software companies, online services and specialist brands frequently pay substantially higher commissions than general retail marketplaces because their margins and customer lifetime values differ. The best choice depends on the audience rather than on the headline commission rate alone.

Direct partnerships can also become viable once a website has demonstrated consistent traffic and sales. Smaller manufacturers sometimes negotiate customised arrangements that are unavailable through large affiliate networks, reducing dependence on standard programme-wide rate [tables]({{ 'tables/' | relative_url }}).

That said, beginners should not switch programmes purely because another merchant advertises a higher percentage. A retailer with poor conversion, weak customer trust or limited product availability may still earn less overall than Amazon. The goal is diversification based on total revenue, not simply chasing the highest advertised commission.

## The lasting lesson for affiliate websites

The importance of the 2020 commission cuts extends beyond the specific percentages that changed. They became a widely cited example of platform risk in affiliate marketing.

For beginners, Amazon Associates remains a useful benchmark for learning affiliate fundamentals, testing buyer intent and understanding how commercial content performs. However, the events of 2020 showed that long-term businesses should avoid assuming today's commission structure will still exist tomorrow.

A healthier affiliate website gradually reduces its dependence on any single merchant by expanding into additional retailers, relevant affiliate networks and, where appropriate, direct brand partnerships. That approach does not eliminate commercial risk, but it makes one company's policy change far less likely to determine the future of the entire business.

    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
